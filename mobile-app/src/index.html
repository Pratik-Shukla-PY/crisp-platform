Privacy & Safety

  • No personal identity collected. We never ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • Faces in photos are blurred on your phone before upload — the server never sees raw faces.
  • Reports are linked only to a pseudonymous device code (SHA-256 hash) that you alone can see.
  • We process reports based on your consent. You can withdraw consent any time by deleting your data.
  • Reports are kept only while the crisis response needs them; deleted reports are permanently removed within 24 hours.
  • Categories of recipients: crisis responders, UNDP analysts, AI verification (Anthropic), encrypted storage (AWS), maps (OSM/Mapbox), messaging (Telegram/WhatsApp/Twilio for SMS+IVR). Data may be processed in countries other than yours.
  • Your rights: access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction. Contact privacy@crisplabs.tech for any privacy concern.

    Privacy & how it works

    Why CRISP

    Anonymous

    Reports are linked to a private device ID that we cannot trace back to you. No phone number, no email, no login needed. You can use the app forever without revealing who you are.

    Offline-first

    The app works in low-connectivity zones. Reports queue locally on your device and upload automatically once the network is back. You never lose what you've captured.

    Faces blurred

    Faces are detected and blurred on your phone before the photo is uploaded. The server never sees an unredacted face. This is a UNDP requirement (§2.9) and a CRISP guarantee.

    What we collect

    • Photos you take (with faces blurred client-side)
    • Voice notes you record
    • Text descriptions you type
    • GPS coordinates at the time of capture
    • Crisis type, infrastructure type, and damage classification you select
    • A SHA-256 hash of your device fingerprint (your reporter ID)

    What we never collect

    • Faces (blurred on-device before upload)
    • Your real name
    • Your phone number or email (unless you choose to sign in)
    • Browser fingerprints, tracking pixels, or advertising IDs
    • Your contacts, photos library, or any data outside what you explicitly submit

    Your rights

    • Delete any single report (per-report Delete button on the detail view).
    • Delete all your reports at once (Settings → Delete all my reports).
    • 24-hour soft-delete grace — you can undo deletion within 24 hours before permanent removal.
    • Export your reports as JSON / CSV (Settings → Export my data — coming soon).
    • Anonymous reports stay anonymous forever; we cannot unblind them.
    • To correct a report, delete it (you have a 24-hour undo window) and submit a corrected one. In-place editing is on our roadmap.
    • Object to / restrict processing of a specific report by emailing the contact at the bottom of this page.
    • Withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal triggers deletion of all your reports.

    Lawful basis

    We process your reports on the basis of your consent, given when you first tap "Accept & continue" on the home screen. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data; we will not retain reports after withdrawal.

    Where your data goes

    CRISP shares your reports only with the categories of recipients listed below, all under contractual or commercial data-protection terms. Data may be processed in countries other than yours.

    • Crisis responders & UNDP analysts — to triage and act on the report.
    • AI verification (Anthropic Claude) — multi-modal truth-scoring of your photo against satellite imagery; processed in the US.
    • Encrypted storage (AWS S3) — region per deployment; default ap-south-1 (India) for UNDP submissions, with server-side encryption at rest.
    • Maps & building footprints (OpenStreetMap / Mapbox) — to render the location picker; only the bounding box of your area is shared.
    • Messaging gateways (Twilio for SMS / IVR; Telegram & WhatsApp for chat ingestion) — only if you report through those channels.
    • Satellite imagery providers (ESA Sentinel-2, Microsoft Planetary Computer, ESA Copernicus) — receive only the bounding box around the reported location, never your photo or device code.

    Automated decisions & AI

    CRISP uses AI (Anthropic Claude) to verify your report against satellite imagery and assign a truth score, and to suggest classifications for voice and SMS reports. These automated assessments only help responders prioritise — they never block, reject, or delete your report, and a human analyst reviews reports before action is taken. You can request human review of any automated assessment by emailing privacy@crisplabs.tech.

    How long we keep your data

    Reports are kept only for as long as the active crisis response requires (default retention is indefinite while the crisis is open; analyst review may close a report sooner). When you request deletion, your reports are queued for hard-delete and permanently removed within 24 hours. Photos and audio attached to deleted reports are erased on the same schedule. Anonymous aggregate statistics (counts, regional totals) may be retained as non-personal data after deletion — these never identify you.

    Open source

    CRISP is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence. The code is published on GitHub. You can audit how your data is handled, propose changes, or self-host the platform.

      Tap Send. If you're offline, the report queues and uploads automatically when the network is back.

    Multi-channel parity

    You can report through the PWA (this app), the Android app, an SMS, an IVR call (voice phone tree), a WhatsApp message, or a Telegram chat. Every channel produces the same report on the responder side, with the same protections.

    Children & vulnerable users

    CRISP is designed for adult reporters. Children under 16 should report through a trusted adult, parent, or community responder. We take particular care in the processing of personal data of vulnerable categories (people in displacement, marginalised groups, victims of violence) — the same anonymous device-code model applies, and we will never publish identifying details. If you are reporting on behalf of someone vulnerable, the same protections apply.

    Contact us

    For any privacy question — access, correction, restriction, objection, or a privacy concern — email privacy@crisplabs.tech. We respond within a reasonable time. For suspected data breaches please email breach@crisplabs.tech. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may further contact the UNDP Chief Data Officer through the UNDP transparency portal at undp.org.
