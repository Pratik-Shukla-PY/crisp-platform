The Challenge
The First 48 Hours Are Critical
After a sudden-onset disaster, accurate damage data is the difference between life and death. But the people who need help the most often have the least technology. CRISP bridges this gap.

The Problem

Existing platforms require smartphones, internet, and technical literacy. Billions of people in disaster-prone areas have none of these. Voice calls and SMS are their only lifeline.

Our Solution

Accept reports from any device (smartphone, feature phone, landline), in any language, at any bandwidth. AI verifies each report using satellite imagery, nearby corroboration, and voice analysis.

The Impact

Control centers get verified, actionable intelligence within minutes. AI separates real emergencies from false alarms. Every genuine report gets a response.

Intelligence Engine
AI-Powered Assessment
Claude (Haiku by default; Sonnet available for enterprise tiers) cross-verifies every signal on analyst request. No single data point drives the decision. Source-aware scoring ensures fair treatment for all reporters.

Multi-Signal Cross-Verification

The AI doesn't just look at photos. It cross-checks text urgency, satellite imagery, nearby corroboration, and caller identity — adapting weights based on the source channel.

Photo Evidence

Does the photo show real damage? Does it match the text description? Could it be a stock photo?

25%

Text & Voice

Specific details, emotional distress, local knowledge that only someone on-ground would know.

25-40%

Satellite Imagery

Sentinel-2 + Maxar compared against ground photos. Detects flooding, structural damage, fire scars.

25%

Corroboration

Multiple independent reports from the same area? That's the strongest signal of a real incident.

25%

Source-Aware Fairness: IVR & SMS

Callers without smartphones cannot send photos. CRISP never penalizes them for it. For voice calls and SMS, text/voice content weight increases to 40%, and a traceable phone number is treated as evidence of sincerity. These are often the most vulnerable populations — their reports get fair scores.

How It Works
From Report to Rescue
1

Report Arrives

Via any of 6 channels. Audio transcribed by Whisper (any language to English). Location geocoded by Claude AI + Mapbox. Media chunked into 1KB packets with DJB2 checksum.

2

Dashboard Shows It

Report appears on the Leaflet map with source badge (App/Telegram/WhatsApp/SMS/Call). Control center sees location, description, audio players, and caller info.

3

Admin Runs AI Assessment

One analyst click. Claude (Haiku) analyzes all signals: text, photo, satellite, nearby reports, caller identity. Returns confidence score (0-100) + recommended action. Cached per-report so subsequent views cost nothing.

4

Decision & Dispatch

Send Help / Mark False / Delete. Export PDF report for rescue team with photos, satellite images, and AI analysis. All in under 60 seconds.

Trust & Safety
Security by Design
18 vulnerabilities identified and fixed. Zero registration barrier. Privacy-first architecture.

Zero Registration

No accounts, no passwords. Anyone can report. Device identified by anonymous hash.

Admin Auth

Admin endpoints protected by API key. Field reporters need zero authentication.

XSS Prevention

All user input HTML-escaped before rendering. No innerHTML injection possible.

Rate Limiting

30 req/min per device. Prevents flooding and DoS attacks.

CORS Restricted

Only configured origins allowed. No wildcard access.

CSP Headers

Content Security Policy, X-Frame-Options, X-XSS-Protection via nginx.
